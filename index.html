Department: Mechanical and Metallurgical Engineering

Course: Metallurgical Engineering

Elementum Prime symbolizes the future of innovation, resilience, and transformation. Although I initially envisioned a career in electrical engineering, the rapid growth of digital technology in Namibia during 2023 sparked a strong fascination with computers, modern systems, and problem-solving.

This growing interest ultimately guided me toward the field of Metallurgical Engineering, where science, engineering, and industrial innovation intersect. In 2025, I began pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Metallurgical Engineering at the University of Namibia, developing expertise in materials processing, extraction technologies, and sustainable industrial development.

Much like metallurgy itself, my journey is rooted in transformation — refining raw potential into strength, precision, and meaningful innovation.

Registered under student credential 225097834 within the Department of Mechanical and Metallurgical Engineering at the Ongwediva JEDS Campus of the University of Namibia, with an academic focus centered on raw mineral processing, alloy characterization, metallurgical systems analysis, and thermodynamic process optimization.
